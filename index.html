Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to shipments booked through TMS GLOBAL INTERNATIONAL COURIER SERVICES.

  1. The sender must provide complete sender and receiver addresses, valid telephone numbers and the correct PIN code. The sender is responsible for failures caused by incorrect or incomplete details.
  2. TMS GLOBAL is not liable for returns caused by an incorrect address, inability to contact the addressee or similar reasons.
  3. The sender must provide one primary identity proof (such as Aadhaar) and one secondary identity proof (such as Voter ID, PAN card or driving licence), as required.
  4. If a post-acceptance check finds that actual or volumetric weight exceeds the declared weight, the difference in charges will be collected from the customer.
  5. Vacuum packing, if requested or required, is charged separately per article.
  6. Return-to-Origin (RTO) courier service is charged extra.
  7. Delivery on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays is not available for services where the carrier does not operate on those days.
  8. Delivery dates are estimates, not guarantees. Delivery depends on destination and service-provider availability.
  9. Additional charges apply where the delivery location is classified as a remote area.
  10. Address-change or redirection charges after booking are payable by the customer.
  11. TMS GLOBAL's liability for damage during delivery is subject to the applicable carrier terms and law.
  12. Shipments must not contain hazardous, prohibited or restricted materials. Items with a limited shelf life are not recommended.
  13. Customs duties, indirect taxes and other destination import charges are payable by the consignee, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  14. Charges are based on whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight.
  15. Medicine shipments must include a doctor's prescription and a pharmacy invoice bearing the pharmacy stamp. Destination and carrier restrictions apply.
  16. For a lost shipment, any liability of TMS GLOBAL is limited to USD 100 or the value declared at booking, whichever is lower, subject to applicable law and carrier terms.
  17. Customers are advised to purchase insurance for shipments valued above USD 100. Free insurance is not included unless confirmed in writing.
  18. The sender agrees that TMS GLOBAL, its service providers and government authorities, including customs and security agencies, may open and inspect shipments where required or permitted.
  19. Delays caused by customs inspection, local security requirements, weather or other events beyond TMS GLOBAL's control are not included in quoted transit times.
  20. Courier complaints should be raised within 48 hours of delivery, with supporting details. Claims remain subject to applicable law and carrier terms.
  21. Subject to applicable law, disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the competent courts in Ludhiana, Punjab.

Additional Terms & Conditions for Fragile Items

  • Shipments containing bottles, glassware or other fragile items are packed with due care. However, damage, breakage or leakage may occur during transit; any liability or compensation is subject to the applicable carrier terms and law.
  • Any damage or leakage should be reported promptly with photographs, and the original packaging should be retained for inspection. Claims, replacements or refunds are subject to carrier conditions and applicable law.
  • By accepting delivery, the recipient should inspect fragile items promptly and report visible damage within the applicable claim-reporting period.
  • Destination customs clearance, duties and import restrictions are the consignee's responsibility. The sender and consignee must cooperate with the carrier and customs authorities if an item is held or delayed.

These terms operate alongside the applicable service provider conditions and destination-country laws. Applicable law will prevail where it conflicts with these terms.
